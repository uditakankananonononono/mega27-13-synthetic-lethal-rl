This analysis occurs AFTER the negative exploratory third-order test and is descriptive artifact QC, not preregistered hit testing. Script `scripts/guide_position_qc.py` and full gene/position matrix `results/gse154112-guide-position-qc.json`.

Among 15 single-gene targets represented in each of three positions, across-target median of each gene's max-minus-min position-specific median log2(D26/D9) is 0.419 in D26 replicate 1 and 0.504 in replicate 2. Maximum across-gene position ranges are 1.683 and 2.186 log2 units. Eight all-dummy control constructs have median log2(D26/D9) +0.579 and +1.154. Distinct-gene target-load strata have median read-count growth 0 targets: +0.579/+1.154; 1 target: +0.423/+0.427 (353 constructs); 2 targets: +0.100/+0.116 (4689); 3 targets: -0.171/-0.184 (18719). These groups differ in gene composition and construct structure, so the gradient is NOT an isolated causal guide-load effect. But position effects and load association make a naive guide-bootstrap contrast fragile, and neither can be dismissed by a raw-ranked triplet. Gene/position matrix can inform a blocked-position sensitivity test; that test is not yet run. No q<0.05 triple under the earlier BH analysis; no cancer selectivity or measured cell viability.
