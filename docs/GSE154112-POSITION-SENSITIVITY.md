# Exploratory position-matched sensitivity after initial negative
2026-09-26. Motivated by the previously documented position artifact, then run on the same already-inspected GSE154112 three-way workbook. NOT a pre-outcome prospective test, not a blocked bootstrap or formal statistical inference. Complete code and all 455 triple records in `scripts/position_matched_sensitivity.py` and `results/gse154112-position-matched-sensitivity.json`. Source: https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE154nnn/GSE154112/suppl/ ; workbook SHA256 b54507687f8f5d945eac15c6452cdc3a85bf2faee7ed14f126268c8fd656d889.

For each triple, each of six target-to-position permutations gets an inclusion-exclusion contrast built from its matched triple, three matched pairs, three matched singles and all-dummy configuration. Per-configuration median across guides, same D9 baseline and D26 biological replicates as original. 453 triples have all six permutations; two have five (missing one exact matched configuration). Median of within-triplet max-minus-min position-layout contrasts is 1.943 log2 and 2.124 log2 across replicate 1 and 2. ZERO of 455 have all available position layouts negative in both replicates. The original raw-ranked MTOR/TOP1/TYMS mean contrasts remain negative on average but have position-layout ranges 1.950/2.469 and are not robustly negative across all layouts. This stringent descriptive sign criterion is not a controlled false discovery test and absence of a robust sign is not evidence of absence of interactions. The original BH analysis already yielded zero q<0.05, and this reinforces the need for orthogonal validation rather than rescuing a candidate. No healthy viability or multi-model replication.
