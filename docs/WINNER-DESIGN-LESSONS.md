# Prior ISEF winner design lessons, not a claim of equivalence
Owner asked 2026-09-26 to take inspiration from earlier ISEF winners, naming Natasha's project. Verified primary Society for Science profile https://www.societyforscience.org/regeneron-sts/2024-student-finalists/natasha-kulviwat/ and official 2023 ISEF award list https://www.societyforscience.org/press-release/regeneron-isef-full-awards-2023/ : Natasha Kulviwat's claudin-5/blood-brain-barrier suicide pathogenesis study received the Gordon E. Moore Award at ISEF 2023; the 2024 STS finalist profile reports de-identified tissue measurements of claudin-5, albumin and IgG plus genetic mediators. ISEF abstract https://abstracts.societyforscience.org/Home/FullAbstract?AllAbstracts=False&Category=Biomedical+and+Health+Sciences&FairCountry=Any+Country&FairState=Any+State&ISEFYears=0%2C&ProjectId=23691 . These are distinct competition events; avoid calling the 2024 STS profile an ISEF 2024 award.

Transferable design pattern: a narrow biological mechanism plus more than one kind of independent measurement and a clearly testable disease-context interpretation. Here that means source-separated knockout response, independent cell context, plausible repair/state mechanism, calibrated uncertainty, and matched nonmalignant perturbation if selective vulnerability is claimed. The existing 2019 monogenic fit + one negative ovarian triple count screen do not meet that evidentiary pattern. A winner analogy is a critique rubric, not a substitute for a discovery or a guarantee of an award.
