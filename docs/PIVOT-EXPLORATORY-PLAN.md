# Exploratory pivot after original HGSOC higher-order gate failed
Dated 2026-09-26. This plan follows visible first numerical rows in all three TRACS files and the PEO1 count workbook. It is NOT pre-outcome preregistration and cannot serve as confirmatory validation. Original locked HGSOC pair/triple/normal-selectivity gates remain unfulfilled and untouched. A true future confirmation needs another lab's still-unseen, eligible experiment or new wet-lab study.

Narrow question: can a source-separated DNA-repair-gene ranker trained on development-only HGSOC monogenic screens prioritize condition-specific loss of HGSOC suspension/spheroid fitness in an independent PEO1 guide-count assay? This is an exploratory method benchmark, not synthetic lethality, not a normal-selective cancer discovery, and not an external pair/triple outcome.

Sources: independent-lab PEO1 GSE123290 https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE123nnn/GSE123290/suppl/ , 2019 raw guide-count workbook checksum 6aab064fe3d66ce76848b9f547876498b84d8aa852f0e814b8a354e75390a26d; iOvCa147 GSE150246 https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE150nnn/GSE150246/suppl/ and OVCAR8/TOV1946 GSE190294 https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE190nnn/GSE190294/suppl/ , connected method/lab, gene-level TRACS outputs. Historical SCORE HGSOC-identity-filtered 8-line monogenic training matrix and Reactome repair network are development only. The initial three TRACS rows and PEO1 first guide row were printed in source audit, so source outcomes have already been touched; no claim of prospective untouched validation.

PEO1 assay guard before scoring: verify sample design in primary paper and SRA/GEO sample metadata, count depth and replicate concordance. GeneID embedded guide-to-gene mapping; drop multi-targeting/ambiguous identifiers; filter low baseline counts by a fixed rule (e.g., median Ctrl >=20, at least two guides/gene); library/timepoint batch cannot be silently pooled. The primary published comparison is S10 vs A10, not S5 vs A5: use median-guide log2(S10+1)/(A10+1) after library-depth normalization and baseline-count filtering; S10 has only two replicates while A10 has three, so do not manufacture three matched pairs. Day-5 S5 vs A5 is a sensitivity check, not another independent dataset. Ctrl is the common starting/baseline condition, not a condition-matched day-10 control; it may guide baseline filtering or separate within-arm depletion, not a nonexistent matched Ctrl correction. The publication explicitly prioritized A10/S10. Permute gene labels preserving guide multiplicity and compare to simple mean single-gene dependency, repair-pathway-degree, network-neighbor, and random rankers; evaluate top-k gain over strongest baseline with paired bootstrap by gene and BH where multiple k/cutoffs are explored. Do not tune on PEO1 and then call it external. Null/negative results stay documented and trigger a separately labelled pivot, never a threshold rescue.

Candidate novelty requires a target not already reported in source paper or older literature; independent assay confirmation, mechanism beyond pathway co-membership, and a fair win are separate gates. A computational rank alone is NOT a newly discovered vulnerability. If PEO1 mapping/QC lacks comparable signal or the ranker fails strong baselines, report it as failed exploratory pivot and seek a new source/endpoint; no positive claim from it.
