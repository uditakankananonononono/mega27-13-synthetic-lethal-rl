# SPIDR selected cross-context descriptive audit freeze

Frozen 2026-10-07 before calculating distributions or correlations from the selected XLSX values. Prior exposure includes the article's aggregate overlap statements, headline interactions, and top full-screen score rows. This is not a blind validation or new discovery experiment.

Question: how do reported interaction-score signs and ranks vary across the three contexts within the 1,165 pairs selected by the original authors using RPE1? This is a selection-conditioned description, not transport performance over the full genome, normal selectivity or a policy benchmark.

Input: exact Supplementary Table 4 bytes/hash recorded in `results/spidr-source-audit.json`. Use all 1,165 pair rows if identities are unique and all three named score columns are finite. Stop on missing, duplicated or nonfinite values; do not impute. Parse score columns by exact names, not order. Pair rows remain source order for audit and alphabetical for any output, never intervention ranking.

Fixed output: per-context minimum/median/maximum, negative-sign counts, sign disagreement counts for each context pair, average-tie Spearman correlations, and descriptive counts below the source-reported -1 score threshold. The source score threshold is not our significance or viability threshold. No p-values, bootstrap CIs, FDR, new hit calls, cancer-normal comparison or policy training. Counts are not independent datasets. Do not choose a new cutoff after inspecting these outputs.

Limits: RPE1 ascertainment truncates its score distribution. HeLa/K562 follow-up is conditional on that selection. Different CRISPRi effectors and backgrounds can contribute to context shifts. Aggregate scores do not supply replicate-level uncertainty. Known source pairs get no novelty credit. Keep all original scientific gates unmet.
