# Histotype guard for the legacy monogenic matrix (2026-09-26)
Sanger Project SCORE historical matrix maps 29 rows to Broad names ending `_OVARY` (via Broad naming.csv). That suffix is an anatomical/lineage label, NOT proof of high-grade serous histology. The current 25-model training proxy (four rows held out) therefore must be called an ovarian-mixed-histotype development proxy, not an HGSOC-trained model.

Independently checked identities and exact model mapping: `ACH-000524 KURAMOCHI_OVARY` = Cellosaurus Kuramochi CVCL_1345, explicitly high-grade ovarian serous adenocarcinoma, https://www.cellosaurus.org/CVCL_1345 ; `ACH-000696 OVCAR8_OVARY` = OVCAR-8 CVCL_1629, same Cellosaurus histology, https://www.cellosaurus.org/CVCL_1629 ; `ACH-000001 NIHOVCAR3_OVARY` = OVCAR-3 CVCL_0465, same histology, https://www.cellosaurus.org/CVCL_0465 . KURAMOCHI and OVCAR8 were ALREADY held out of the current proxy, leaving OVCAR-3 as one verified HGSOC training line. That is not enough for the >=3 model builder and thus an HGSOC-only fit is blocked rather than inferred.

`ACH-001630 PEO1_OVARY` and `ACH-001632 PEO4_OVARY` are also held out. Their Cellosaurus entries https://www.cellosaurus.org/CVCL_2686 and https://www.cellosaurus.org/CVCL_2690 label ovarian cystadenocarcinoma and hereditary BRCA2 context without explicitly stating high-grade serous in the displayed Disease field. Other papers may classify them more specifically, but they do not change the current development set. Remaining 24 legacy ovarian-named lines have NOT all been individually histotyped and cannot be counted as verified HGSOC. A conservative `allowed_models` gate is now available in `build_legacy_cancer_proxy` for a future source-verified model roster; no post-hoc identity assumption or successful HGSOC-only fit is claimed. See method caution https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0072162 .
