# Post-outcome empirical shift sensitivity, not assay power

A focused ChatGPT critique at https://chatgpt.com/c/WEB:296c5c37-e326-4265-a4dc-7d57a4c3fbb8 suggested an additive-null triple residual with planted controls. That contrast already existed in the preregistered exploratory plan and subsequent position-matched analysis. The suggestion to learn triple viability from monogenic Project SCORE data was **not** adopted: those data contain no measured triple effect or matched normal viability. The arbitrary AUROC >0.8 and Pearson <0.7 gates in that external response are not data-derived thresholds and are not adopted.

A useful, narrower addition is `scripts/position_injection_diagnostic.py`: it reads the original GSE154112 guide-count workbook, independently calculates each existing third-order, position-matched log2-count contrast, and reports the uniform *triple-only* downward log2-CPM shift needed to make every available position layout strictly negative in both biological replicates. This is a deterministic sensitivity calculation, not injected physical data, a learned model, a prospective power analysis, a positive-control recovery claim, or a significant result. Its null control is zero shift, and the unit tests use known artificial contrasts and an exact-boundary failure case. Source: https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE154nnn/GSE154112/suppl/ ; workbook SHA256 `b54507687f8f5d945eac15c6452cdc3a85bf2faee7ed14f126268c8fd656d889`.

Running all 455 observed triples: 0 meet the stringent all-layout/both-replicate negative sign gate at shift 0. Artificial uniform shifts of 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 log2 units would pass 21, 127, 391 and 454 triples respectively. The median threshold is 1.300 log2 units; the remaining outlier at 4 illustrates heterogeneity. In contrast, the weaker mean-across-layouts sign gate passes 111 at shift 0. This broad susceptibility to an artificial shift shows that a sign-gate alone does not control false discovery, not that any shifted triple would validate. The script's 455 zero-shift signs and layout counts match the earlier independent position-sensitivity output exactly; 48 engineering tests pass. Full per-triple reproducibility data are in `results/gse154112-position-injection-diagnostic.json`.

This diagnostic does not make a pair/triple synthetic-lethal discovery, resolve guide efficiencies or normal-cell preservation, rescue the RL-versus-greedy/beam loss, resolve PEO1 QC, or satisfy the requested fair external benchmark beat. It is a concrete audit tool and a reason to keep the locked biological claim blocked. It is post-outcome and cannot be claimed as an independent validation.
