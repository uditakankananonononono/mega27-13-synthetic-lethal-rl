# Synthetic-lethal RL in high-grade serous ovarian carcinoma (HGSOC): preregistration v1
Locked 2026-09-26 before inspecting any outcome tables or fitting any model. Source reconnaissance of release notes and paper descriptions is not outcome inspection. This is a computational research plan, not evidence that a discovered interaction exists. Every deviation needs a dated, pre-outcome amendment; after seeing validation outcomes, label a change exploratory.

## Question and biological scope
Can sequential reinforcement-learning search of DNA-repair genetic interactions in HGSOC nominate previously unreported pairs or three-gene vulnerabilities with independent experimental support and a defensible mechanism? HGSOC (not pan-cancer) and homologous-recombination / replication-stress / alternative end-joining DNA repair are fixed before data ingest. A cancer-specific effect requires evidence relative to a nonmalignant comparison, not merely ovarian cell-line depletion. In HGSOC, BRCA1/2 and HRD status are stratification variables, not automatic evidence that any putative pair is novel.

## Data separation and provenance
A. Development: DepMap single-gene Chronos dependency scores, omics and lineage metadata (freeze one release and file checksums before loading values), plus pathway and protein-interaction network. Remove all evaluation cell lines from learned simulator parameters and policy optimization. B. Exclusion list: published synthetic-lethal pairs from SynLethDB and primary literature screened before nominations. C. External pair test: a distinct study/lab's dual-guide CRISPR readouts with effect sizes and replicate structure; do not use its results in simulation design, policy training, ranking or threshold selection. D. External triple test: distinct triple-guide perturbation measurements including all singles, all doubles, and triple on the same cell model, with controls. A study with only drug-treatment synergy or observational co-dependency cannot prove a three-way synthetic-lethal claim. E. Healthy-cell benchmark: matched nonmalignant perturbation viability is preferred. GTEx normal tissue expression is a biological plausibility proxy, NOT knockout viability, and cannot alone substantiate cancer selectivity. If no valid matched healthy perturbation resource exists, label selectivity unvalidated.

Data split is by cell-line and by source laboratory to prevent leakage, with gene-family and pair-held-out stress tests. Study identifiers and blocked identities are fixed before outcome loading. Computational patient TCGA/GDC cross-checks are supportive, not independent knockouts. Avoid using an independent test paper's proposed hits as simulator features. Never count rows as datasets or metadata accessions as experiments. Only source-verified, individually processed accession-level datasets count toward the program's aspirational 120+ gate; if meaningful count is smaller, report the smaller number.

## Simulator and causal limits
Build a context-conditioned graph of at least 2,000 measured genes after coverage QC, with annotated DNA repair modules and cell-specific mutation/copy-number/expression features. A calibrated single-knockout viability model is fit on development lines only. Pair and triple viability are predictions from explicit probabilistic interaction terms constrained by pathway topology and any DEVELOPMENT-only combinatorial experiments; without such evidence, higher-order effects are not identified from monogenic screens. Propagate uncertainty via ensembles and abstain above an out-of-domain threshold fixed in development. A healthy-state simulator trained on normal expression alone remains a surrogate and cannot justify healthy-cell preservation. Benchmark each simulator's held-out single-gene effects, calibration, and (if available) dev-only dual-guide effects before policy use. If simulator fails gate, pivot internally to active selection and uncertainty-focused biological mapping, not a discovery claim.

## Agent and baselines
State: cell context, graph embedding, active knockout set (0 to 3), budget, simulator uncertainty. Actions: add one gene from prespecified eligible repair-network module; episode ends at 2 or 3 knockouts or budget exhaustion. Mask invalid/repeated selections, avoid known pairs in discovery pool. Reward: predicted cancer viability reduction minus healthy viability reduction, penalties for single-knockout essentiality, uncertainty, off-target/core-essentiality and experiment cost. Training uses simulator only; external results never provide reward. Compare RL to matched-budget random, greedy one-step, network-neighbor search, beam search and Bayesian/uncertainty-guided search; run >=10 fixed seeds. Report wall clock, unique eligible tested combinations and exact compute budget. Policy victory requires superior independent hit enrichment over strongest baseline, with paired permutation p<0.05 and corrected multiple testing where relevant; a high simulator score alone cannot win.

## Locked validation, nomenclature and threshold
For pairs, assess external measured interaction beyond an explicit multiplicative independent-effects null and vs published dual-guide experiment controls, assay QC passing, direction concordance and confidence interval excluding null after BH-FDR q<0.05. Require >=2 distinct models including an HGSOC model for any general HGSOC claim, else restrict to the tested model. For triples, require all seven nonempty subsets measured in the same experiment: each singleton and pair above the experiment's predefined viability survival threshold (default normalized viability >=0.8 if paper does not supply one), triple below <=0.3, and a negative third-order contrast with 95% CI excluding 0, multiple-testing-corrected q<0.05. These thresholds are conservative provisional assay-based rules; if source measurements are not comparable, triple discovery status is impossible, not imputed. Existing known hits are excluded from novelty credit using a date-frozen registry and literature search; absence in a database means 'not found in searched sources', never proven never reported. Candidate gene names and gene IDs, evidence, confidence, source accessions, exclusion search timestamp, and reasons for failure go into an immutable candidate ledger before external outcome joins. Independent confirmation of model-predicted interactions on published data is retrospective support, not a new wet-lab discovery.

Mechanism: preregister DNA repair pathway mapping (Reactome/GO), HGSOC HRD stratification, expression/alteration context, epistasis-null comparison, alternative explanations (copy-number artifact, paralogs, global essentiality, growth rate). Require >=1 direct functional mechanism experiment from independent literature or report mechanism as hypothesis only. Do not infer immune evasion mechanism from this DNA-repair project.

## Failure branches and integrity
If the RL policy does not outperform matched-cost non-RL search on external measured interactions, report it as negative and pivot within this repo to characterizing why repair-network priors failed or to calibrated active-experiment prioritization, with new pre-result gates for new data. If no independent triple screen covers a nominated combination, report unvalidated triples separately; do not call them discoveries. If no healthy viability benchmark exists, drop the selective-vulnerability claim. No clinical prescription. No ChatGPT judge round while program-wide pause remains. Archive full seed outcomes, unsuccessful nominations, source hashes, and amendments. Do not inflate tool count: only independently executed research tools with logged input/output count toward the aspirational 40; code paths/libraries are not 40 tools. Paper target ~20 substantive pages with Times New Roman and >=10 correctly used numbered equations when evidence exists, not filler. Source code, tests and hashes are required, but test pass counts are not biological success.
