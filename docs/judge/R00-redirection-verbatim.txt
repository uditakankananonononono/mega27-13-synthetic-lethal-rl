Initial assessment of the stalled premise

Given the constraints you list, the original hypothesis is currently not an externally testable synthetic-lethal discovery project. The limiting factor is not the RL algorithm; it is the absence of a measurement substrate.

The preregistered gate appears to require something like:

HGSOC-specific genetic interaction → selective tumor vulnerability → preserved normal-cell viability → reproducible across models.

The available public evidence instead supports:

Monogenic dependency modeling (Project SCORE-like resources)

Drug-response modeling

Expression/state biomarker discovery

Pathway-level vulnerability hypotheses

It does not support discovery claims for:

validated pair/triple synthetic lethal interactions,

tumor-normal selective combinatorial effects,

causal higher-order genetic interactions.

The GSE154112 result is especially damaging because it is not merely “small”; it is an attempted higher-order screen where the measured triples apparently do not produce a detectable corrected signal. That makes “better RL” unlikely to rescue the original framing.
