Judge verdict

The central validity threat is task–feature non-identifiability, not lack of compute. The experiment asks a sequential learner to predict held-out synthetic-lethal pairs, but gives every method only three coarse guide-library/design categories. Those categories are not mechanistic representations of pair compatibility, gene dependency, context, or interaction structure. The negative result therefore establishes that these category-only policies did not outperform the specified baselines on this split; it does not strongly test whether learning can predict synthetic lethality from richer biological information.

That distinction matters because the observed result is internally consistent: random is best, RL is below random, and the corrected degree-normalized policy is also below random. That is evidence against the current representation/strategy, not against synthetic-lethal prediction generally.

The other major threats are secondary:

Pair split rather than gene-disjoint split. The same genes can occur in development and evaluation in different pairs. This can create information leakage for richer future models, although it is less damaging for the current three-category representation.

Dataset shift. The Achilles-category frequency changes dramatically between development and evaluation. This makes the frozen category strategy particularly vulnerable to covariate shift.

Evaluation dependence. Shared pairs, cell-line structure and lineage structure mean the stated bootstrap intervals should not be interpreted as independent-observation confidence intervals.

Retrospective protocol exposure. The exposed S4/S5 rows are a protocol-integrity issue, but because the outcomes were not repeatedly queried and the protocol was otherwise frozen, I would not treat that as the primary reason for the negative result.

Source uncertainty. The unresolved fractional S3 matrix and processing provenance appropriately prevent treating it as a clean quantitative benchmark.

Importantly, none of these justifies changing the biological gate.

2. One implementable novelty improvement

I would implement exactly one substantive improvement:

Gene-disjoint, dependency-profile contextual acquisition

Keep the biological endpoint and hit definition completely unchanged. Replace the three design-category features with a pre-outcome gene representation derived only from development-eligible public data, and require the acquisition policy to generalize to genes it has never seen during training.

The key novelty is not "use AI for synthetic lethality." That is not novel enough.

The methodological novelty would be:

Can sequential synthetic-lethal acquisition generalize to completely unseen genes when gene representations encode their dependency landscape rather than merely their guide-library category?

4. New biological hypothesis?

None is established by these results.

I would explicitly reject attempts to convert the category-frequency shift into a biological discovery.

The observation

Achilles: 8.8%→2.1%

and

paralog: 5.3%→11.8%

is a dataset/design distribution shift, not evidence that paralog interactions are biologically enriched in some new HGSOC mechanism.

Likewise, the failed ovarian single-gene biomarker track cannot establish absence of a biological effect because your own power analysis indicates inadequate ability to detect the proposed −0.3 shift under that multiplicity.

And the Harle dataset is not HGSOC with matched nonmalignant controls.

Therefore:

No new biological hypothesis is justified as an established finding.

A future hypothesis could be prospectively generated from an independent, mechanistically grounded representation—for example, whether dependency-context similarity predicts context-specific combinatorial vulnerability—but that would be a new hypothesis to test, not a conclusion from the present experiment.

I would keep that distinction very strict.

Bottom line

The strongest scientifically defensible statement is roughly:

On the specified Harle public benchmark, three guide-library design categories were insufficient for the tested sequential search policies to outperform random acquisition; REINFORCE performed worse than random, and a degree-normalized correction did not reverse that result. This does not establish absence of synthetic-lethal structure, because the representation contains little direct biological information and the benchmark is neither HGSOC-specific nor matched-normal.

The one improvement worth spending effort on is therefore not another RL variant. It is a frozen biological gene representation + genuinely gene-disjoint acquisition experiment, followed by an independently sourced, gene/pair/line-disjoint external test.

If that experiment also fails against random/greedy, the negative result becomes substantially more informative: you would have evidence that the added public biological context does not provide transferable predictive signal under the locked endpoint—not merely that one particular RL formulation failed.
