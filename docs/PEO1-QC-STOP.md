# PEO1 source QC stop before any ranker result
GSE123290 raw workbook https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE123nnn/GSE123290/suppl/ , SHA256 6aab064fe3d66ce76848b9f547876498b84d8aa852f0e814b8a354e75390a26d. Official study https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6710300/ prioritizes A10 vs S10. Descriptive QC script/results are `scripts/peo1_qc.py` and `results/peo1-guide-qc.json`; no candidate scoring or model benchmark has run.

119,461 guide rows; strict single-symbol parser retains 111,314, excluding 8,147 multi-gene/ambiguous identifiers (many `#`-separated targets; not silently assigned to first gene). Sample totals range about 24.3M-32.9M counts, but sample medians vary sharply despite similar totals: A10_A/A10_B ~169/173, A10_C 2; S10_A 121, S10_B 33; Ctrl_A/B 3/6, Ctrl_C 183. For single-symbol guides passing Ctrl median >=20 and >=2 guides/gene, raw log2-count Pearson correlations are A10_A-B 0.311, A10_A-C 0.246, A10_B-C 0.244, S10_A-B 0.299. These are lower than expected for a clean replicable guide library and may reflect sample/library composition, indexing/schema errors or protocol properties. They DO NOT establish a causal assay defect without reconciliation. The low-baseline filter itself can induce misleading Ctrl anticorrelation; do not interpret filtered Ctrl correlations as library quality.

STOP: do not benchmark a source-transferred model or call an anoikis vulnerability until sample identity, guide library A/B mapping, depth-normalized replicate QC, day-10 design and within-gene concordance are reconciled to the paper or an independent processor. A10/S10 not interchangeable with day-5. The original locked synthetic-lethality gate remains separate and failed. This exploratory pivot can be abandoned without inventing a result.

Partition investigation (script `scripts/peo1_library_audit.py`, result `results/peo1-library-partition-qc.json`): GeneID prefixes divide 63,762 `HGLibA` and 55,448 `HGLibB` rows plus 251 other. Addgene's source https://www.addgene.org/pooled-library/zhang-human-gecko-v2/ confirms GeCKO v2 A and B are two distinct half-libraries with ~3 guides/gene each. They cannot be presumed equally represented in every sample. Indeed `HGLibA` median counts are near zero in A5 triplicates and S10 duplicates while `HGLibB` medians are high there; `HGLibB` is near zero in Ctrl_A and A10_C, while `HGLibA` remains high in Ctrl_C. This strongly suggests unequal half-library mixture/sample representation, but does NOT prove which biological samples were pooled, mislabeled or meant to be compared. Summing A+B naively masks it. No source-faithful day-10 comparison until library-specific compatible subsets and paper metadata are reconciled. These QC findings are methodological cautions, not a cancer discovery.
