# GSE154112 exploratory triple-depletion result: no statistical hit
2026-09-26. Separate pre-outcome exploratory analysis plan committed at d2caab3dcac7fb6c2e4ef6d66525971275e046fc (SHA-256 aea8f1f1369ddec1749313844d128dbadd90ce76ca515b422b6f845644fa30e4). This is NOT the project preregistration gate and no RL model was trained/evaluated.

Source: https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE154nnn/GSE154112/suppl/ , GSE154112_Three-way_ovarian_cancer_gRNA_screen_count_per_million_reads.xlsx SHA256 b54507687f8f5d945eac15c6452cdc3a85bf2faee7ed14f126268c8fd656d889. Read-count data only, one OVCAR8-ADR cell line, no healthy tissue, 15 genes, two D26 biological replicates.

Method: 32,768 complete guide-configuration rows; 4,725 rows carrying repeated same-gene targets excluded from distinct-gene subset comparisons. 576 unique control/single/double/triple distinct-gene sets remain (1+15+105+455). Every distinct triple had all seven constituent nonempty subsets and no-target control with >=4 guide combinations. log2((D26 CPM+1)/(D9 CPM+1)) computed per D26 replicate; control-referenced third-order inclusion-exclusion effect assembled from medians of guide constructs. Guide bootstrap 1,000 resamples with fixed seed 20260926, two-sided sign-proportion p, BH correction over 455 triplets. This bootstrap cannot account fully for guide-position effects or biological replicate variance and must not be interpreted as definitive significance.

Result: both replicate effect signs agree for 198/455 triplets. Seven raw negative guide-bootstrap 95% intervals excluded zero, but after 455-way BH correction ZERO q<0.05. Minimum q~0.9992. The most negative raw contrast MTOR/TOP1/TYMS is -1.174 log2 (replicate effects -1.123/-1.226), guide-bootstrap interval [-2.346,-0.083], unadjusted p~0.042, BH q~0.9992; its rank was selected after viewing outcomes. It is NOT an experimentally validated triple synergy or novel synthetic lethal candidate. The no-target control median D26/D9 log2 growth is +0.579/+1.154; median effects drift lower with perturbation-set size, raising artifact/guide-load concerns. No ranker baseline or external independent validation has been run.

Verdict: exploratory negative, no supported three-way candidate on this assay. Preserve all rows and dead ends. Possible next question is whether pairwise HGSOC DNA-repair interactions can be prioritized from multiple truly independent assays; this would need a NEW prospective design and cannot overwrite or rescue the locked original gate. Claiming previously unrecognized selective cancer vulnerabilities remains blocked by absent matched healthy perturbation outcomes.
