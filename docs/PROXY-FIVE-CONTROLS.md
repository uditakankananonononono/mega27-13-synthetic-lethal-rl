# Development-only search sanity check with five controls
2026-09-26. Same 2019 Project SCORE monogenic + Reactome arbitrary prior as the earlier proxy study. Ten seeds and 300 same-surrogate calls per method. All methods exclude 591 historical SynLethDB registry pairs in the 349-gene action space. RL: 200 learning calls + 100 proposals; compare BEST of all 300 queried triples to avoid discarding RL's learning calls. Random: 300 uniform legal triples. Greedy/beam: 30 random starts, then 270 one-gene mutations of best or rotating top five. Network: neighbor-biased legal triples (pathway co-membership). Uncertainty: probabilistic low-monogenic-uncertainty sampling. Proposals that repeat still consume calls. Controls are transparently simple and not sophisticated optimized algorithm implementations.

Mean best same-surrogate reward, ten seeds: RL 0.77345; random 0.77164; greedy 0.80275; beam 0.79943; network 0.77729; uncertainty sampler 0.77973. Respectively random/greedy/beam/network/uncertainty beat RL in 5/10, 10/10, 10/10, 8/10, 9/10 seeds (strict >). The earlier 2/10 RL versus random value evaluated only its 100 post-training proposals, not the 200 training episodes. Both comparisons retained as distinct estimands; their difference is not a biological insight. The strongest simple control beats RL in every seed. See full per-seed results/cancer-only-proxy-five-controls.json and scripts/compare_more_baselines.py. No independent pair/triple outcomes, no matched normal viability, no HGSOC subtype filtering on training data. Circular simulator reward is NOT interaction evidence.
